# Motion

Use this for transitions, state changes, menus, drawers, modals, realtime updates, and visual polish.

## Rules

- Motion must clarify cause and effect.
- Animate transform and opacity, not layout properties.
- Keep durations short for operational tools.
- Respect reduced motion preferences when the stack supports it.
- Do not let motion block repeated work.

## Checks

- Hover, focus, active, loading, and disabled states feel intentional.
- Menus and drawers reveal directionally.
- Realtime updates do not cause layout jumps.
