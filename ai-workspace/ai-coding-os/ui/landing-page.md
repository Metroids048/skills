# Landing Page

Use this for product, brand, venue, portfolio, or offer pages.

## First Viewport

- The brand, product, place, object, person, or literal offer must be visible immediately.
- H1 should be the name or offer category, not a vague value prop.
- Supporting copy explains value.
- Show a real product, state, image, or interactive scene. Avoid generic gradients.
- Leave a hint of the next section visible on mobile and desktop.

## Structure

- Hero with real signal.
- Proof or product view.
- Key workflow or use cases.
- Differentiation.
- CTA with clear next step.

## Checks

- No stock-feeling hero.
- No split text/media card hero.
- No decorative-only image.
- Mobile text does not overlap or clip.
